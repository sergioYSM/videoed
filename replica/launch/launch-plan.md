# Launch plan: Ownframe

## Before launch (gates)
- [ ] **Auto-captions run for real** in Chrome, Edge and Safari with real speech (Spanish and English). Until then, the landing must not promise them as tested.
- [ ] Test on a real iPhone (Safari 17+) and a mid-range Android: import, edit, export, file size limits, memory.
- [ ] Trademark/domain checks for "Ownframe" (brand.md).
- [ ] Terms and privacy written plainly (no licence over user content; list processors: host, analytics, Stripe later). Lawyer review.
- [ ] The free promise published on the site (pricing.md).
- [ ] Pro features are built before Pro is sold (4K, backups, brand kit). Until then: "próximamente", no checkout.
- [ ] Analytics without cookies (Plausible) and error tracking (Sentry) live: **never send file names or media**, only counts and error types.

## Beta
- Waitlist / beta list on the landing (email only, double opt-in).
- 20-50 creators for two weeks, desktop and phone. Ask in writing before quoting anyone.
- Key metric: "videos exported per active user per week"; second: export failures per 100 exports.

## Where the people are (check each community's self-promotion rules first)
- r/VideoEditing, r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube, r/TikTokCreators: threads about paywalls, captions limits and terms of service. Help first; mention the tool only where allowed.
- Privacy communities (r/privacy, r/degoogle): the "nothing is uploaded" architecture, explained honestly with its limits.
- Spanish-speaking creators: editing-tutorial YouTubers and Discords.
- Clipsong's users (same audience): Creador+ bundle.

## Launch posts
- **Show HN**: "A video editor that never uploads your footage: WebCodecs + OPFS in the browser". Lead with the architecture, the export pipeline, the numbers (10 s of 1080×1920 in 3.4 s), and what does not work yet.
- **Product Hunt**: "Ownframe: edit short videos without handing them over". First lines: nothing uploaded, captions without a monthly cap, no watermark, free stays free.
- No ads or posts naming the original. No comparison page without a lawyer.

## First 10 people to talk to by hand
1-4. Creators who posted about paywall changes or caption limits in the last months (find them in the communities above).
5-6. People who film clients or minors (teachers, therapists, coaches): privacy is a requirement for them.
7. A journalist or researcher who handles sensitive footage.
8. A small brand's social media manager (Pro: brand kit).
9. A creator on a low-end Android phone (performance limits).
10. A Clipsong beta user (bundle).

Ask: what did you use last week, what broke, would "never uploaded" change what you film, would you pay 4 €/month for 4K + backups.
